### Prompt Structure

You are tasked with generating a Python implementation of the n-body problem for simulating the orbits of Jovian planets under mutual gravitational forces. The code must adhere to the following specifications:

1. **Language**: Write the solution in pure Python.
2. **Command-line Argument Handling**: Accept `N` (number of time steps) as a command-line argument, e.g., `python script.py 1000`.
3. **Symplectic Integrator**: Implement an order-2 symplectic integrator where velocities are updated first, followed by positions.
4. **Momentum Conservation**: Adjust the Sun’s initial velocity so that the total momentum of the system is zero (denoted as `offsetMomentum`).
5. **Output Requirements**:
   - Print the initial and final energy in %.9f format (e.g., `Initial Energy: 123456789.0000000`, `Final Energy: 123456789.0000000`).
6. **Precision**: Ensure numerical stability and accuracy to validate with `ndiff -abserr 1.0e-8` for N=1000.
7. **Physics Constants**:
   - Use the provided constants: `PI`, `SOLAR_MASS`, and `DAYS_PER_YEAR`.
   - Gravitational force is proportional to \( \frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^3} \), where \( G = 4\pi^2 \) (since \( G = \frac{4\pi^2}{DAYS\_PER\_YEAR^2} \)).

**Decomposition for Chain-of-Thought**:
- First, derive the gravitational force formula between two bodies using the given constants.
- Explain how to compute total momentum and adjust the Sun’s velocity (`offsetMomentum`) to enforce zero net momentum.
- Outline the symplectic integrator steps: update velocities first (using central differences), then positions (using forward Euler).
- Discuss energy conservation checks, including kinetic and potential energy contributions.

**Code Structure**:
- Define a class `JovianSystem` with methods for:
  - Computing gravitational forces (`compute_forces`).
  - Updating velocities (`update_velocities`).
  - Updating positions (`update_positions`).
  - Calculating total energy (kinetic + potential) at each step.
- Use a loop to iterate `N` times, applying the symplectic integrator.

### Key Requirements

- **Symplectic Integrator Order**: Velocities → Positions (no mixed updates).
- **Momentum Adjustment**: Compute `offsetMomentum` as the negative of total initial momentum divided by Sun’s mass.
- **Energy Output**: Print formatted energy values with 9 decimal places.
- **Precision**: Avoid floating-point errors beyond \(10^{-8}\) for N=1000 validation.
