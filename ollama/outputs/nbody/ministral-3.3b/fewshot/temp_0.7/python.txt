### Notes
1. **Ambiguity in Integrator Design:**
   - The symplectic integrator structure should clarify whether velocities (`v`) are updated as position derivatives (explicitly `v = r / dt`) or included in a modified acceleration formula.
   - Averaging accelerations over steps may be necessary to avoid divergence near close encounters.

2. **Efficiency Pitfalls:**
   - The problem requires O(N²) force computations per step; avoid redundant calculations when updating kinetic/potential energy. Use vectorized operations where possible (e.g., sum pairwise forces).

3. **Momentum Correction:**
   - Offsetting Sun’s momentum requires solving for `vx_sun` such that:
     ```
     Σ mass_i * v_total = 0
     ```
   - The correction term should be added *before* energy calculations but after initial state assignment.

4. **Precision Handling:**
   - The target LLM must avoid rounding constants or initial velocities; maintain 16-20 decimal digits in intermediate steps to meet `ndiff` validation (e.g., use Python’s `Decimal` for force calculations).

5. **Output Validation:**
   - The prompt ensures energy output includes both *kinetic* and *potential*, but must explicitly separate them per the problem’s "total energy" requirement.
